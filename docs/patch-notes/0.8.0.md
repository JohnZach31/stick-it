# Stick-It v0.8.0 — Cut It Out

**Release date:** not released (status: **development**)

## TL;DR
Photos learned a new trick: cut out a person, dog, building or object right on your device, tidy the edges by hand, and stick it straight onto the board. Desktop users can right-click the board to add things, and the corkboard gains receipts, tickets, postcards, photo strips and a particularly silly bowl of alphabet soup.

## Highlights
- **Real cutouts.** Pick a photo, let Stick-It cut the subject out (people, pets, buildings, products, plants), then Erase or Restore bits and soften the edge. Your original photo is never touched, and your photo never leaves your device to make a cutout.
- **Sticker and mounted looks.** A thin white scissor line like a real sticker, or glue the cutout on light cardboard, kraft, white paper, notebook paper or graph paper.
- **Right-click to add.** On a computer, right-click empty board for a small menu: note, photo, cutout photo, receipt, ticket, postcard, photo strip, recording, video. On a phone, tap the desk and choose More.
- **New scraps.** Receipts and tickets (with an honest "decorative numbers only" promise), postcards you can turn over, instant-film photo strips made from photos already on your board.
- **Alphabet Soup.** A Premium cosmetic: a note's words float as pasta letters. Everyone can see it; only Premium accounts can make it (nothing to buy yet).
- **Working together, quietly.** Small initials show who is on a shared board, a note someone is editing says so, and comments and a lightweight "changes requested / ready for review" step stick to objects, never to the empty board.

## Added
- On-device cutout engine (U²-Net family, WebAssembly), Cutout Maker with Erase, Restore, Edges, brush size, zoom and pan, local undo/redo, Retry (finer edges on the second try) and a clear failure state.
- Receipt, Ticket, Postcard and Photo Strip objects, each with a few restrained variants.
- Photo strips from 2-6 existing photos (Make photo strip), or from files; a small strip editor (order, add, remove, caption).
- Alphabet Soup cosmetic (Premium, enforced by the server as well as the app).
- Right-click insertion menu and object menus; "Comment…" in object menus on shared boards.
- Presence initials, active-object badges, an expiring "someone is editing this note" lock.
- Comments (object-attached) and review state, with a paper-slip thread.
- Three typewriter fonts for receipts and tickets (Cutive Mono, Courier Prime, Special Elite), self-hosted.

## Improved
- Board thumbnails and shared/exported pictures draw real cutouts by their outline.
- Postcards in shared views turn over when tapped.
- Editor role on shared boards is now recognised correctly in "Move to board".
- Guest boards now bring receipts, postcards, strips and cutouts along when signing in.

## Changed
- The desktop hint now says "Right-click anywhere to add something" (double-click still makes a note).
- `script-src` gained `'wasm-unsafe-eval'` (needed for WebAssembly; it does not allow JavaScript `eval`).

## Fixed
- A duplicated cutout now draws as soon as its copy exists.
- First-run cutout failures show the "Couldn't make a clean cutout" panel instead of a half-empty editor.

## Collaboration
- Presence uses a private Realtime channel per board (`board:<id>`), limited to that board's members by database policy.
- Comments: editors and owners write, everyone on the board reads. Review: owners request changes and resolve; editors mark ready for review.

## Privacy & Security
- Cutouts never leave the device. No provider, no API key, no secret in the browser.
- New policy text: comments, review state and presence are listed in the Privacy Policy and storage notice (English and Hebrew).
- Every new text field is plain text and always written with `textContent`; control characters and bidi overrides are stripped; shared views are sanitised the same way.
- The Premium cosmetic is checked by the database, not only by the app.

## Under the Hood
- `js/cutout.js` (engine + tested maths), `js/cutout-maker.js` (editor), `js/sticker.js` (contour and card materials), `js/objects.js` (rules for the new scraps, unit-tested), `js/collab.js` (presence rules unit-tested, comments, review).
- Cutouts are stored as their own `cutout` asset linked to the original (`source_asset_id`) or, for guests, a blob in IndexedDB; Cutout → Polaroid → Cutout never reruns the model.
- Migrations: `20261001120000_paper_objects.sql` (strip pictures counted as the object's assets), `20261001130000_premium_cosmetics.sql`, `20261001140000_collab.sql` (channel authorization, `object_reviews`, `set_review_state`, `board_collab_summary`).
- `tools/dev-server.mjs`: a tiny static server with correct MIME types (Python's mis-serves `.mjs`).

## Known Limitations
- Cluttered or ambiguous scenes do not cut out cleanly; the editor says so and offers manual tools or "Use original".
- Cutouts run single-threaded in WebAssembly: about 2-3 s (Quick) and 6-12 s (Finer, first use downloads 44 MB).
- Touch editing (finger Erase/Restore, two-finger pan and pinch) is built and checked in an emulated phone viewport; it has not been tried on a physical device.
- Presence and the soft lock are verified with a local two-tab transport and unit tests; the live Supabase Realtime transport and its authorization policy have not been exercised against a real project yet.
- Strip pictures are reordered with arrow buttons, not by dragging.
- The "someone is editing" lock covers note text and the text fields of the new scraps, not photo captions.
- Alphabet Soup animates only a few notes at a time and shows at most the first 100 characters as pieces (the words themselves are never changed).
