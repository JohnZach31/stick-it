# Stick-It v0.8.0 — Cut It Out

**Release date:** not released (status: **development**)

## TL;DR
Photos learned a new trick: cut out a person, dog, building or object right on your device, tidy the edges by hand, and stick it straight onto the board. Desktop users can right-click the board to add things, and the corkboard gains receipts, tickets, postcards, photo strips and a particularly silly bowl of alphabet soup.

## Highlights
- **Real cutouts.** Pick a photo, let Stick-It cut the subject out (people, pets, buildings, products, plants), then Erase, Restore or Refine the edge. The cutout fills the editor so you can see what you're doing, and your original photo is never touched. Your photo never leaves your device to make a cutout.
- **Sticker and mounted looks.** A white scissor border (none, thin or medium), or the cutout glued onto light cardboard, kraft, white paper, notebook paper or graph paper.
- **Right-click to add.** On a computer, right-click empty board for a small menu: note, photo, cutout photo, receipt, ticket, postcard, photo strip, recording, video, alphabet soup. On a phone, tap the desk and choose More.
- **New scraps.** Receipts and tickets (with an honest "decorative numbers only" promise), postcards you can turn over, instant-film photo strips made from photos already on your board.
- **Alphabet Soup.** A Premium cosmetic: a real bowl of soup with your words floating in it as pasta letters. Everyone can see it; only Premium accounts can make it (nothing to buy yet).
- **A Done pile.** Finished notes slide into a small stack at the edge of the board instead of cluttering it. Read them, put them back, or throw them away.
- **Working together, quietly.** Small initials show who is on a shared board, a note someone is editing says so, and comments and a lightweight "changes requested / ready for review" step stick to objects, never to the empty board.

## Added
- On-device cutout engine (U²-Net family, WebAssembly) and the Cutout Maker: a big canvas that frames the subject, Erase, Restore, Refine (smooth, soften, trim), brush size, zoom, pan, Fit subject / Fit original, local undo/redo, a white-border choice, Retry (a second look) and a clear failure state.
- Receipt, Ticket, Postcard and Photo Strip objects, each with a few restrained variants.
- Photo strips from 2-6 existing photos (Make photo strip, with a small picker if you need to choose), or from files; a strip editor (order, add, remove, caption).
- Alphabet Soup cosmetic (Premium, enforced by the server as well as the app).
- Right-click insertion menu and object menus; "Comment…" in object menus on shared boards.
- Presence initials, active-object badges, an expiring "someone is editing this note" lock.
- Comments (object-attached, on a small clipped paper slip) and review state, with a "Request changes" slip.
- Done pile: Mark done on a note, a Done · N stack at the board's edge, a tray to read, put back or delete.
- Focus Mode formatting strip: bold, italic, headings, highlight, lists, checklist and link without opening the "…" menu.
- Recordings show elapsed / duration and a waveform that fills as it plays; recordings and videos can be resized; videos play right on the board (and can still be opened large).
- Drag the highlighted window on the minimap to pan the board.
- Captions and small labels use a short list of clear, readable handwriting-style fonts for every script (note fonts are unchanged).
- Three typewriter fonts for receipts and tickets (Cutive Mono, Courier Prime, Special Elite), self-hosted.

## Improved
- While one object is selected the rest of the board stays still (no lifting, no swaying), and the selected object stays on top.
- Clicking the text of a selected receipt, ticket or postcard edits it, and the text cursor appears where you can type.
- Boards with many photos use much less memory: pictures are drawn from small previews while the originals stay untouched.
- Board thumbnails and shared/exported pictures draw real cutouts by their outline.
- Postcards in shared views turn over when tapped.
- Editor role on shared boards is now recognised correctly in "Move to board".
- Guest boards now bring receipts, postcards, strips and cutouts along when signing in.

## Changed
- The desktop hint now says "Right-click anywhere to add something" (double-click still makes a note).
- `script-src` gained `'wasm-unsafe-eval'` (needed for WebAssembly; it does not allow JavaScript `eval`).
- Questions and problems use Stick-It's own dialogs everywhere; no browser pop-ups remain.

## Fixed
- A duplicated cutout now draws as soon as its copy exists.
- First-run cutout failures show the "Couldn't make a clean cutout" panel instead of a half-empty editor.
- Dragging a note no longer rebuilds the whole minimap on every pixel of movement.

## Collaboration
- Presence uses a private Realtime channel per board (`board:<id>`), limited to that board's members by database policy.
- Comments: editors and owners write, everyone on the board reads. Review: owners request changes and resolve; editors mark ready for review.

## Privacy & Security
- Cutouts never leave the device. No provider, no API key, no secret in the browser.
- New policy text: comments, review state and presence are listed in the Privacy Policy and storage notice (English and Hebrew).
- Every new text field is plain text and always written with `textContent`; control characters and bidi overrides are stripped; shared views are sanitised the same way.
- The Premium cosmetic is checked by the database, not only by the app.
- Finished notes keep who finished them (signed-in people only) and when, stored with the note.

## Under the Hood
- `js/cutout.js` (engine + tested maths), `js/cutout-maker.js` (editor), `js/sticker.js` (border and card materials), `js/objects.js` (rules for the new scraps, unit-tested), `js/collab.js` (presence rules unit-tested, comments, review).
- Cutouts are stored as their own `cutout` asset linked to the original (`source_asset_id`) or, for guests, a blob in IndexedDB; Cutout → Polaroid → Cutout never reruns the model.
- Mask clean-up keeps real gaps (an arch, a lattice) and fills accidental holes, smooths solid edges but leaves thin detail alone (unit-tested).
- Migrations: `20261001120000_paper_objects.sql` (strip pictures counted as the object's assets), `20261001130000_premium_cosmetics.sql`, `20261001140000_collab.sql` (channel authorization, `object_reviews`, `set_review_state`, `board_collab_summary`), `20261001150000_owner_premium.sql`.
- `tools/dev-server.mjs`: a tiny static server with correct MIME types (Python's mis-serves `.mjs`). `tools/perf-fixture.js`: a local board of about 55 objects for before/after performance numbers.

## Known Limitations
- Cluttered or ambiguous scenes do not cut out cleanly; the editor says so and offers manual tools or "Use original".
- Cutouts run single-threaded in WebAssembly: about 2-3 seconds for the first try.
- Touch editing (finger Erase/Restore, two-finger pan and pinch) is built and checked in an emulated phone viewport; it has not been tried on a physical device.
- Presence and the soft lock are verified with a local two-tab transport and unit tests; the live Supabase Realtime transport and its authorization policy have not been exercised against a real project yet.
- Strip pictures are reordered with arrow buttons, not by dragging.
- The "someone is editing" lock covers note text and the text fields of the new scraps, not photo captions.
- Alphabet Soup shows letters for the first 100 characters (fewer in a small bowl); the full words are always in the note and in Focus Mode.
- Finished notes still count toward a board's object limit.
