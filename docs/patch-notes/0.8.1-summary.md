# Stick-It v0.8.1 — One Place

## TL;DR
v0.8.1 is the "tidy the house" release after v0.8.0. Settings, account, privacy, shortcuts and legal now live in one Control Center; notes gained a quick done tick, trimming and resizing; the board gained a Clean up button and a status slip; comments and legal pages stopped looking like prototypes. Status is still **development**. Nothing is pushed.

## Already completed before this patch
Not redone here (v0.8.0 baseline): Cutout Maker redesign, subject auto-fit, mask refinement and border control, caption-safe fonts, the Alphabet Soup bowl and its animation cap, the Request Changes slip, the Done pile and its tray, the Focus Mode formatting strip, the photo-strip modal, quiet hover while selecting, audio progress and resize, video resize and inline playback, the draggable minimap, image-preview memory savings, dark mode and mobile support.

## What was added in 0.8.1
- 🆕 Control Center (Account, Appearance, Sharing, Privacy & Data, Sounds, Shortcuts, Legal & About) with a left rail / phone tab strip.
- 🆕 One Save and one Cancel for staged settings; immediate actions stay immediate.
- 🆕 Sounds section (switch, volume, preview) stored as a preference.
- 🆕 Searchable Shortcuts cheat-sheet.
- 🆕 Clean up (my screen / whole canvas) in the top bar, undoable.
- 🆕 Status slip at the foot of the canvas.
- 🆕 Quick done tick beside the x.
- 🆕 Fit paper to content, Rip off empty paper, Restore full paper.
- 🆕 Corner handle to resize a note (v0.8.0 had no note resize at all).
- 🆕 Comment tab on the object's bottom edge and a compact thread slip.
- 🆕 Spiral-notebook legal pages (English and Hebrew, light and dark).
- 🆕 Structured patch data for the website (`patch-notes.json`, generated `patch-notes.js`).

## What was fixed
- Typing in Bio rebuilt the profile picture. Reproduced (the picture was redrawn on every keystroke), fixed, measured: zero changes to the picture's DOM while typing a bio or a name.
- Pasted text came in highlighted and a line too low. Reproduced with browser-style clipboard HTML: a transparent background was read as a highlight and a hidden line break was kept. Fixed.
- Alphabet Soup showed a strip of washi tape.
- Comment rows mixed author, time, delete and text. Rebuilt.
- Premium and photos were inconsistent. What the bug actually was: (1) the crown existed only on the header avatar, while the profile screen showed Premium as plain words; (2) your own comments showed an initial instead of your picture; (3) the older single-screen account view used only the sign-in provider's photo, never an uploaded one (that view is no longer reachable when signed in to the cloud). All three now follow one rule.

## What was improved
- Email-updates row copy cut to one line.
- Active Shares, comments and the username check use the Stick-It loader, with Try again on failure.
- Account numbers and sign-ins load only when a section that shows them opens; Shortcuts, Legal and Sounds build when first opened.
- A selected note's ✕ is visible without hovering.

## What remains partial
- Loading consistency: footer slip, local loaders and button loaders are in; board switching and first sign-in still use the full-screen loader (the page reloads).
- Accessibility: tabs, labels, focus and Esc are in place and the keyboard works; no screen reader was run and the reduced-motion setting was not toggled in a browser.
- The Control Center was exercised against a local stand-in for the account service (real migrations), not the real project.
- Other people's pictures next to comments: initials only (by design).

## What is deferred
- Board locks, polls, timers and reactions (v0.8.2+), the sound library (v0.8.3), payments, e-mail OTP in production.
- Sleeping far-offscreen notes: investigated, not needed (see below), so not done.

## Findings worth knowing
- Username availability was already real: `handle_available` RPC (authenticated only, answers true/false), the unique index remains the final authority, a taken name on Save is handled (`HANDLE_TAKEN`), and SQL tests cover it. Kept as it was; added a loader.
- Offscreen cost: a board of 211 objects has about 5,700 DOM nodes (about 27 per object) and a heap of 10.6 MB; dragging a note costs about 28 ms per 120 moves versus 23 ms on 55 objects. Soup animation pauses off screen. Nothing there justified a risky rewrite.
- Control Center cost: opens in about 7 ms with 191 elements; Shortcuts adds about 214 when first opened; typing 30 characters in the shortcut search takes under 1 ms.

## What I can test right now
1. Click the avatar, the gear and the keyboard button: one dialog, three different sections. Try the arrow keys on the section list.
2. Type quickly in Bio: the picture must not flicker. Type a username: "Checking…" then "✓ Available".
3. Change dark mode in Appearance, press Cancel: it reverts. Change it again, press Save: it stays.
4. Sounds: switch it off and on, move the slider, press Play a preview.
5. Shortcuts: search "duplicate".
6. Make a note with two words, then use its ••• menu: Fit paper to content, Rip off empty paper, Restore full paper, then undo. Drag the corner handle of a selected note.
7. Press the ✓ beside the ✕ on a note: it slides to the Done pile; open the pile and put it back.
8. Copy a line from a web page and paste it into a note: no highlight, no extra line.
9. Press Clean up, choose Clean my screen, apply, then undo; repeat with Clean whole canvas.
10. On a shared board: add comments to an object and look at the tab and the slip.
11. Open Legal & About, then each legal page (English and עברית), on a phone width and in dark mode.
12. Open Manage active shares and watch the loader.

## Release blockers
- The legal pages are still DRAFT with open owner markers (unchanged by this patch).
- The Control Center has not been used against the real project (sign-in, Save with real network, real Realtime).
- Same standing blockers as v0.8.0: live Realtime presence, two-device Done-pile sync, touch editing on a physical phone.
