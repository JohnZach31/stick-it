# Stick-It v0.8.3.4 — Lock It Down

Release date: 2026-10-08
Status: **released** (a hotfix release)

A security and reliability hotfix for sharing a board with other people.

## Turning a link off now works
- Turn off an invitation link and the people who got in only through it lose access straight away, even if their page is still open
- People who have another valid link, and anyone you did not add through a link, are never removed
- People already on your board before this update stay on it; only a future, deliberate Turn off can remove anyone
- A page whose access was turned off leaves the board and says so

## Live boards
- Other people's edits show up almost immediately instead of after a short wait
- Stick-It still checks in the background in case a live update is missed

## Your links, in one place
- Collaboration invites are listed under Shared links, next to public links, and in the Collaborate window
- Each shows what it allows, when it was made, how many times it was used and who joined, with Copy and Turn off
- Links made before this update appear too, marked as made before tracking
- Guests who join without an account get a friendly name such as "Leaflet #184"
- "Join with invite link" in the board list takes a pasted link

## Fixes and polish
- Long invite links no longer stretch the dialog
- Error messages are compact, dismissible and carry a short code (see docs/dev/ERROR-CODES.md)
- Age screen: Sign out and Continue share a row, with calmer Month and Year pickers
- Settings > Input has a proper layout and a Stick-It styled choice menu
- Scrolling inside a list or note no longer scrolls the board at its edges, and no longer drags the note
- A cut-off board name shows in full on hover, focus or long press
- A Zone's pin stays above the papers inside it
- Rubik Scribble was retired from the font choices (notes that used it keep working)
- Switching boards is faster: boards are fetched ahead of time while the list is open
