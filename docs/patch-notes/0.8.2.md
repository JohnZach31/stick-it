# Stick-It v0.8.2 — Get a Grip

Release date: not released
Status: **development**

## TL;DR
More control, less friction: pin things in place, name areas of the board with zones, jump anywhere from a command palette, change your own keyboard shortcuts, and trim empty paper the way you meant.

## What changed
v0.8.1 put everything in one place. v0.8.2 lets you hold it still and steer it. Things can be pinned so they stay put, areas of the board can have names, every command can be found by typing, shortcuts are yours to change, and the small annoyances that made the board fight you (paper that wouldn't trim, a tidy-up that counted wrong, a Settings dialog that closed on Save) were fixed.

## New in this patch

### Holding things still
- **Pin in place.** Choose it from a note's menu, or press **P**. A pinned note, photo or scrap shows a small pin, can't be dragged or nudged (even inside a group), is skipped by Clean up (which tidies around it), and can still be selected, edited, resized, marked done, moved to another board or unpinned. Pins survive reloads and sync. Duplicates, pastes and imports are never pinned.
- **Board zones.** *Add a zone* from the board's right-click menu or the command palette. It is a patch of paper behind your notes with a title strip, a corner handle, a material (paper, kraft, cardboard, grid, felt) and a colour. *Move with its notes* (off by default) carries the notes lying on it when you drag it. Deleting a zone only removes the paper; the notes stay. Clean up leaves notes inside a zone where they are.

### Getting around
- **Command palette** (Ctrl/Cmd+K). Type what you want: New note, Pin, Mark done, Clean my screen, Clean the whole board, Bookmark this spot, Search, Settings, What's New, and more. It is built from the same list as the shortcuts, so it always shows the key you currently use.
- **Bookmarks.** Press **B** to save the spot you're looking at, then find it in the palette. *Manage bookmarks* renames or deletes them. Up to 30 per board, kept on this device.
- **Back and Forward.** **Alt+[** and **Alt+]** step through the places you've looked at on a board. A place is remembered once you stay somewhere clearly different for a moment; the list is capped at 30 and lasts for the visit.

### Shortcuts that are yours
- **Change any shortcut.** In Settings → Shortcuts, *Your shortcuts* lists every action that can be rebound. *Change* then press the keys; *Reset* and *Reset all to default* undo it.
- **Safe by design.** A shortcut must include a real key. Keys the browser keeps (Ctrl+T, Ctrl+W…), the system keeps (Alt+Tab), text editing uses (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+Z…) or that have a fixed job (Esc, Enter, Tab, the arrows) are refused with a plain reason. If the keys are already used by another action you get *Replace* or *Cancel*. Esc cancels the prompt only.
- **Saved.** On this device always; when signed in, it follows your account (a small private preferences column, capped at 8 kB).
- **Dark mode.** Key caps are readable in dark mode.

### What's New
- After an update you get one small card: **Show me**, **Not now**, **View patch notes**. *Show me* is a short tour that only points at things that exist on your screen. New people see the normal tutorial instead. *What's New* is always available under Settings → Legal & About and in the command palette.

### Comments
- **Delete.** You can delete your own comment; the board owner can delete anyone's. Other collaborators, viewers and signed-out visitors can't, and that rule is enforced by the database itself. A branded confirmation replaces the browser's own popup. The comment slip is more compact.

### Legal & info
- **Reading options** on every legal page: text size (small, default, large), high contrast, reduce motion, and beige or dark. They are remembered on this device and shared with the app.
- **Beige by default.** The notebook page is beige unless you chose dark (in the app or on the page).
- **Language chooser.** A globe replaces the loose "עברית" link. It lists only languages whose pages are complete (English and Hebrew today) and the same list can serve the app later.
- **One type scale** for all six pages in both languages.

## Fixed
- Clean up could count one set of notes and move another. It now takes one fixed list when you open it and uses it for the count, the move and the undo.
- Fit paper and Rip off empty paper counted trailing blank lines and empty Shift+Enter breaks as content. They now measure the words.
- The "New board links" row lit up its entire row on hover.
- A comment's delete button no longer crowds its text.
- Select-all and the new-note key stopped working for a moment while the shortcut list was being rebuilt; fixed before this patch ships.

## Improved
- **Trim empty paper?** After you stop typing in a note with lots of empty paper, a quiet offer appears (never automatic, once per note per visit).
- **Settings.** Save keeps you where you are and says *Saved*. Appearance has more controls (shadow depth, paper texture, tilt, tape, dots, compact, reduce motion, high contrast) with a live preview; Cancel puts everything back.
- **Esc** closes only the topmost temporary thing: the palette, the tour, comments, the board picker, a menu, the rebinder, a dialog.
- **Sound preview** animates while it plays.
- **Account.** The plan chip opens a short explanation on hover, focus or tap; the storage bar shows used and remaining in words and in the bar; the crown is a crisp vector in dark mode.
- **Buttons** share one quiet hover and press feel and respect reduced motion.
- **Clean up** button is a broom.

## Still being worked on
- The comment-delete rules and the new `ui_prefs` column are migrations that have **not** been applied to the real project yet. Run `npx supabase db push` from the repository; until then deleting a comment and syncing shortcuts will not work against it.
- Bookmarks are per device; they don't sync yet.
- Zones don't have an "arrange inside this zone" action.
- The legal text is still a draft and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.
- Not checked: signed-in plan popover, storage bar and crown against a real account; a physical phone; a screen reader; real Realtime with two accounts; the Done pile across devices.
- The memory audit covered notes, zones, the palette, Settings and the tour. Photos, video, voice memos, cutouts and live collaboration were not measured.

## Under the hood
- One action registry (`defineAction`) now drives the keyboard dispatcher, the palette and the shortcut settings. Pure key rules live in `js/keys.js` and run under Node.
- `delete_comment(uuid)` and a `comments_guard` trigger enforce comment deletion in the database (author, or board owner), including negative tests and four new mutation checks.
- `profile_settings.ui_prefs jsonb` (object only, 8 kB cap, owner-only) carries shortcuts and the seen-update flag.
- Overlay stack: every temporary layer registers once and Esc closes the top one.
- Patch data is generated from `patch-notes.json` into `js/patch-data.js` (version, summary, `tourMode`, tour steps) as well as the website file.
- New migrations: `20261002100000_comment_delete.sql`, `20261003100000_ui_prefs.sql`.
- Tests: SQL/RLS 391, billing/guards 228, client 142, fake backend 41, functions 89, objects 24, collab 18, cutout 26, v0.8.1 guards 49, v0.8.2 DOM and key rules 38, v0.8.2 guards 34. The mutation check catches every mutation.
