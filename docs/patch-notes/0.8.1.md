# Stick-It v0.8.1 — One Place

Release date: not released
Status: **development**

## TL;DR
Settings finally stopped living in six different places: account, appearance, sharing, privacy, sounds and shortcuts now share one roof. Notes get a quick done tick and a way to trim empty paper, and the board gets a tidy-up button.

## What changed
v0.8.0 made Stick-It do more. v0.8.1 makes it feel like one place. The scattered account, settings, shortcut and legal screens became a single Control Center, notes got friendlier hands (a done tick, trimming, resizing), the board learned to tidy itself on request, and a handful of rough edges from v0.8.0 got sanded down.

## New in this patch

### One settings home
- **Control Center.** One dialog for Account, Appearance, Sharing, Privacy & Data, Sounds, Shortcuts and Legal & About. A left rail on computers, a scrolling tab strip on phones, real keyboard-friendly tabs.
- **Opens where you meant.** The avatar opens Account, the gear opens Appearance, the keyboard button opens Shortcuts. Same dialog, no duplicated content.
- **One Save, one Cancel.** Preferences and your profile are staged together. Export, Sign out, Delete account and share-link actions stay immediate.
- **Sounds.** A sound-effects switch, a volume slider and a soft preview. Stick-It doesn't play sounds yet; this remembers your choice for when it does.

### Canvas controls
- **Clean up.** A new button in the top bar. *Clean my screen* tidies only what you're looking at; *Clean whole canvas* tidies everything. It shows how many items it will move, never deletes anything, keeps size and tilt, avoids overlaps, and is one undo step.
- **Status slip.** A small red-note slip at the foot of the board says what Stick-It is up to (syncing, loading photos or media, preparing a share, exporting). It only appears when something is actually slow, and never blocks the board.

### Notes & Done
- **Quick done tick** beside the x on notes and checklists. The note slides into the Done pile.
- **Fit paper to content** shrinks a note around its words. **Rip off empty paper** tears the unused part away (torn, cut or notebook edge) and leaves a small scrap. **Restore full paper** undoes either. All three are undoable.
- **Resize a note** with a quiet corner handle on the selected note. Text is never scaled and never clipped.

### Comments
- A small **Comments · 2** tab hangs off the bottom edge of the object, and the thread opens as a compact paper slip underneath it: avatar, name, time, words, and a quiet delete. Your own comments show your own picture.

### Loading
- Active Shares, comments and the username check use the Stick-It loader (with a Try again when something fails) instead of plain "Loading…".

### Shortcuts
- A searchable cheat-sheet of small key caps, grouped as Selecting & moving, Editing, Board, Media, Navigation and Cutout Maker.

### Legal & info
- The legal pages are written on a spiral-notebook page (ruled lines, margin, binding, sticky-note callouts), in English and Hebrew, light and dark. The words and every disclosure are unchanged.

### Performance
- The Control Center opens in a few milliseconds; Shortcuts, Legal and Sounds are built only when first opened, and account numbers are fetched only when a section that shows them opens.

## Fixed
- Typing in Bio (or Name) no longer rebuilds the profile picture.
- Pasted text no longer arrives highlighted or a line too low (browsers wrap copied text in invisible styling; Stick-It now strips it).
- Alphabet Soup no longer wears a strip of washi tape.
- Comment layout: author, time and delete no longer fight the comment's words.
- Premium looked different in different places (a crown on the header avatar, plain words in the account screen); the crown now sits on the avatar wherever an avatar is large enough.

## Improved
- The email-updates row is one short line: "Occasional Stick-It news and feature updates. You can unsubscribe anytime."
- The profile avatar and the sharing preview are only redrawn when the picture itself changes.
- A selected note's ✕ shows without hovering.

## Still being worked on
- The Control Center was checked against a local stand-in for the account service. It has not been used against the real project yet.
- Other people's pictures don't appear next to their comments (initials only, on purpose).
- Switching boards and first sign-in still use the full-screen loader, because the page reloads.
- Reduced motion and screen readers were not tested in a real browser setting.
- Finished notes still count toward a board's object limit.

## Under the hood
- `openControlCenter` plus a section registry (`CC_SECTIONS`); sections can build lazily. The old Settings sections keep their elements and are moved in, so their listeners are untouched.
- The account form is one closure wired through the shared card; profile edits touch only the text they change.
- Username availability was already server-backed (`handle_available` RPC, unique index as final authority, covered by the SQL tests); it now shows the branded loader while it asks.
- Paste goes through `cleanPastedHtml`; a transparent background is no longer read as a highlight.
- Clean up packs items in reading order into loose rows per page (pure functions, unit-tested).
- A note's height is only ever a minimum (`n.h`), the torn edge is a clip-path from a seeded shape (`n.rip`).
- Structured release data for the future website: `docs/patch-notes/patch-notes.json` (source) and the generated `patch-notes.js`.
