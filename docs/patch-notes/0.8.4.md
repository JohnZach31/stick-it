# Stick-It v0.8.4 — Scratch & Seal

Release date: not yet released
Status: **in development**

Two new ways to keep a note to yourself: a scratch-off foil for notes you would rather not have read at a glance, and a PIN seal for notes that must stay private.

## Incognito notes
- Right-click a note and choose Make Incognito: it gets a silver scratch-off foil. Scratch it with the mouse or a finger; about 60% of the way through, the rest lifts off by itself
- The note's tab stays clear, so you can still drag it like any note. Scratching never moves the note, pans the board or starts a selection
- Works at any zoom and any tilt
- Cover Again puts the foil back; Remove Incognito makes it an ordinary note
- Keyboard and screen reader: the foil is a button; Enter or Space (or Reveal in the menu) lifts it
- It stays Incognito after a refresh and covers itself again every time the board is opened
- Search, previews, the clipboard, print, PDF and board thumbnails show no words for a covered note
- Select several notes and make them all Incognito at once

## PIN-locked notes
- Right-click a note and choose Lock with PIN: choose a PIN of 4 to 8 digits and type it twice. The note becomes a sealed letter with a wax seal and a handwritten "Locked"
- Its colour and size stay; its words and picture are encrypted on your device before anything is saved or synced
- Click the sealed note to enter the PIN. Wrong guesses are slowed down: after three in a row you wait ten seconds, then longer
- Lock Again closes it at any time. Refreshing the page, switching boards or signing out closes every lock
- Change PIN and Remove PIN Lock ask for the current PIN
- Locking several notes at once needs a deliberate confirmation, because they all open with the same PIN
- There is no master PIN and no recovery. If you forget a PIN the note can never be opened again, and Stick-It says so before you lock
- On a signed-in board, the key also depends on a secret that the server only gives out for a correct PIN, and counts wrong guesses for the note, whoever makes them
- Undo and Redo can never bring a locked note's words back

## What the lock does and does not protect
- It protects the words from other people on the board, board owners and administrators, shared links, exports and anyone who copies the stored data
- It does not hide that a locked note exists, where it is, its colour or its size
- A 4 to 8 digit PIN has few possible values. On a signed-in board the server limits guessing; on a board that only lives on this device, only the encryption slows guessing down
- Someone who can read the whole database and the stored note can still try PINs offline. Use a long PIN for anything that matters
- Incognito is a visual cover only. It is not encryption and is never described as security

## Writing
- Ctrl+U (Cmd+U on a Mac) underlines reliably: holding the keys down no longer flips the underline on and off, and the buttons show the same state as the shortcut

## Known limits
- Only plain notes (including checklists) can be Incognito or PIN-locked. Photos, papers, recordings, zones and piles cannot yet
- On a signed-in board, a note with a picture cannot be PIN-locked yet (and a locked note cannot take a picture); remove the picture first. Very large notes are refused too
- Guests who joined with a link cannot create PIN locks; a guest board that only lives on this device can
- A locked note cannot be copied, duplicated, shared or moved to another board
- Real touch scratching has not been tried on a physical phone or tablet yet
- Opening a server-protected lock needs a connection
