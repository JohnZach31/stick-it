# Stick-It v0.8.2.1 — Room to Breathe

Release date: not released
Status: **development** (a PATCH release: not v0.8.3)

## TL;DR
Less chrome, clearer paperwork, and more room for the board.

## What changed

### More board, less browser chrome
- Phones: the header is a single compact row; Search is an icon that opens a full-width field and a Cancel; keyboard-shortcut and help buttons (useless on a phone) are left out. The board starts directly under the header, whatever its height.
- Shared (view-only) boards: one compact status line (board, who shared it, view only) with Report beside it; buttons that do nothing on a shared board are hidden. The row of seven legal links across the bottom is now one small **Legal** chip.
- Desktop: a larger Search bar.
- The Done pile sits about 16px lower.

### Legal, without leaving Stick-It
- Settings → Legal & About → Legal: Privacy, Terms, Young people & parents, Storage, Accessibility, Copyright / DMCA open in a large in-app reader (document tabs, English / עברית, reading options for text size, high contrast and reduced motion, Back to top, dark mode, right-to-left). Esc closes it; focus is trapped and restored.
- **One source of text.** The Markdown drafts in `docs/legal` are the only copy. `python tools/build-legal.py` generates both the standalone pages (`legal/*.html`, unchanged URLs) and `js/legal-content.js` that the reader renders. A test fails if they drift.
- Accuracy pass on the reviewed documents (Privacy, Storage, Accessibility, Copyright / DMCA) and the minimum consistency fix on Young people. All still DRAFT with their owner / legal-review markers.
- A Stick-It language icon (a sticky note with A, א, 中, ع) for the Language control. The inline brand word is semibold, never underlined.

### Piles you can look through
- Arrows on a selected or hovered pile and an index (3 / 24); on touch, tap a pile to browse one paper at a time (arrows, swipe, Back to the pile). Browsing is only a view: nothing in the pile, and nothing synced, changes.

### Links can become media
- Pasting a supported video link (YouTube, Vimeo) asks: **Keep as link** or **Show as embedded video**. A video is a normal board object (resize, move, pin, undo); the original address is kept and shown, and **Show as a normal link** reverses it. Nothing is loaded from the provider until you press Play; the player is removed again when it scrolls out of view or the object is deleted. The player address is built from a validated id, never from the pasted text.

### Small things
- Button states: the generic hover tint no longer overrides a primary button's fill (Save went white-on-cream on hover). Filled buttons now have contrast-checked default / hover / active / focus / disabled tokens in light and dark.

## Not in this patch
Whole-app translation, the custom video player, Fan view for piles, analytics. No change to sync deletion semantics.
