# Stick-It v0.8.3 — Stick Around

Release date: not released
Status: **development** (a feature release)

Things don’t just sit on the board anymore. They get completed, discarded, reacted to, layered, searched and turned into richer scraps.

## Done has somewhere to go
- Done is now a real system space (Boards list → System → Done), with its own warm look and a DONE stamp with the date
- Completed objects keep their original content and state (words, place, layer, angle, pin, reactions, a shopping list’s ticks and prices)
- Put back returns them to the board where they were
- Any paper object can be marked Done (notes, lists, receipts, tickets, postcards, newspapers, clippings)
- A small completion moment: a stamp and a few paper scraps (about half a second); none under reduced motion
- Search it, and filter by Today / This week / Earlier

## Trash without panic
- Deleted objects go to Trash first, with their content, place and when you deleted them
- Restore them (one, several, or all) back where they were, or Delete forever when you really mean it (always asks)
- Trash has its own rougher, greyer look; it is not a shareable board and a public link never shows it

## Shopping List finishing pass
- The Comment tab is now tucked under the receipt’s paper edge in all three looks, and moves, turns and resizes with it
- Done preserves every item’s state; the receipt spacing was reviewed and left as it was

## Newspaper
- A new free scrap: headline, optional subheading or byline, and body text in columns
- Eight looks (Broadsheet, Tabloid, Gazette, Evening paper, Telegraph, Typewritten, Herald, Modern); a new one picks a look at random and you can change it in the menu
- English and Hebrew (right-to-left follows the words); self-hosted fonts only
- An image slot is **not** in this release (follow-up)

## Clippings
- A new scrap for a quote with where it came from: the words, a source title, and a checked source link (domain shown, opens safely)
- Make one from the + menu, from any note (••• → Make clipping), or from the offer after you paste a long piece of text; change or remove the source, or convert it back to a plain note
- The site mark is a letter drawn locally: Stick-It never contacts the source just to show a clipping

## Reactions
- React to anything with 👍 ❤️ 😂 👀 ✅ 💡 from the small + button under a selected object; click a chip to add or remove your own
- Nothing is drawn on an object until it has a reaction; view-only boards can’t react

## Better layering
- Arrange → Bring to front, Bring forward, Send backward, Send to back (also in the command palette, and Bring to front / Send to back for a selection)
- Only the layer changes: never a position, a pile or a zone (zones always stay at the back)

## Search that actually takes you there
- A result count and next / previous (Enter / Shift+Enter) with the result centred and highlighted
- Finds checklist items, shopping items, receipts, newspapers, clippings (text, source title, domain) and papers hidden inside a pile (the pile opens at that paper)
- A scope switch: Board / Done / Trash. (All boards is a follow-up.)

## Better links and video
- Links show a pointer, and the link card uses a letter mark instead of fetching the site’s icon
- Video stays poster-first (nothing is fetched from the provider until you press Play); a playing video has Full screen and Stop
- “Remove the image” is now a different place, shape and label from “delete the note”

## Piles grew up
- Shuffle, move a paper earlier or later, and a fan of up to nine papers on a computer; one paper at a time with arrows and swipe on a phone
- Browsing is still only a view; reorder and shuffle change only the order

## Receipt polish
- A little larger by default, with two or three playful sample lines from ten examples (no OCR — deliberately)

## Also
- Smarter dates: “שישי בערב”, “מחר בבוקר”, “tomorrow evening”, “Friday night” (a part of the day only counts next to a date; the existing chip still asks)
- Account photo: a pencil on the picture and a menu: Upload, Take photo (the camera only starts when you choose it, and always switches off), Remove, Cancel

## Technical notes (for the team)
- **System spaces:** Done and Trash are *views* of the board’s own objects, marked `doneAt` / `trashedAt` on the object itself (so they sync, export and reload like everything else). The sync snapshot is `notes + Done + Trash`; an object is never "missing" because it is done, in Trash, piled or not drawn. Delete forever (the user’s explicit act) is the only thing that removes a row.
- **Collaboration:** unchanged semantics: Done and Trash are board-level states of the object (as Done always was), so a collaborator who trashes something trashes it for the board. Per-user cross-board spaces are a follow-up and need a design decision.
- **Not in this release:** cross-board Done/Trash and "All boards" search, Newspaper image, OAuth providers (Apple / Microsoft need production configuration), link preview thumbnails and show/hide previews (they need a safe fetch path), a completion-effects preference, share-as-sticky from video, Trash auto-expiry (Trash keeps things until you delete them forever; server cleanup applies after Delete forever as before).
