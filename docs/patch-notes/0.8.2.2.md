## Technical notes (for the team)
- **Rotation model:** one stored angle, `rot`, in degrees, range -15..+15 (design range; the sync sanitiser now accepts that range). The natural random tilt a new object gets is only its starting value; turning edits the same number. Nothing is added at render time, so there is no second random angle, no compounding and no drift. Shift snaps to 5 degrees; Arrange has Rotate left / right (3 degrees) and Straighten (0). A copy or paste keeps its source's angle (it used to get a fresh random one).
- **Supports rotation:** notes (including checklists), receipts, tickets, postcards, photo strips, shopping lists, photos. **Does not:** zones, piles, embedded video, audio and video recordings. Pinned items do not turn.
- **Pin:** only `pinned` is stored. The tack's spot and tilt come from the object's id; the drop and fall are temporary animations. Duplicates keep their existing semantics (a copy is never pinned). Move to another board keeps the pin.
