# Accessibility statement (DRAFT)

> **Draft for review. This is not a certificate of conformance.** [LEGAL REVIEW RECOMMENDED]

Last updated: 2026-10-01

Stick-It is a personal project by Jonathan Zachevsky, Israel. Stick-It aims to meet **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** as its product-development accessibility target. Where Israeli accessibility requirements apply to Stick-It, we also aim to meet the applicable requirements of Israeli Standard SI 5568 and the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities regulations. These are separate references: SI 5568 is not simply the same thing as WCAG 2.2. **We have not been audited by an accredited accessibility specialist and do not claim certification or verified conformance.**

## What we have done

- Keyboard use: dialogs trap and restore focus, `N` creates a note, visible focus rings.
- Skip link, labelled controls, reduced-motion support.
- Colour contrast checked for the main text and button colours in light and dark themes.
- Self-hosted fonts including Hebrew and Arabic scripts; Hebrew pages are right-to-left.

## Known limitations

- Notes are positioned by dragging. Keyboard movement exists for a selected note, but selecting and arranging notes without a pointer is limited.
- Voice-memo and video controls have not been tested with assistive technology.
- Not every status message is announced to screen readers.
- Not tested with NVDA, JAWS or VoiceOver by disabled users, and not tested beyond 200% zoom.
- Not yet tested on physical phones and tablets with assistive technology.
- Pile browsing, embedded video and the in-app legal reader are new and have only had developer checks, not testing with assistive technology.
