## Reporting copyright infringement

Stick-It lets people create, paste, upload and share content. Users are responsible for making sure they have the rights or permission needed for what they add. Stick-It does not proactively inspect every private board for copyright ownership. If a valid copyright complaint is received, Stick-It may investigate and may restrict or remove the reported material where appropriate. This page does not limit any responsibility Stick-It has under the law. If you are a copyright owner (or authorised to act for one) and you believe content on Stick-It infringes your copyright, please send a notice to the general copyright contact below. ### What your notice must include

A written notice should contain: (1) your physical or electronic signature; (2) identification of the copyrighted work you say was infringed; (3) identification of the material and **where it is** (for Stick-It, the share link or page address); (4) your contact details (address, telephone, e-mail); (5) a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law; and (6) a statement, **under penalty of perjury**, that the information is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.

Please be accurate. Knowingly misrepresenting that material is infringing can carry legal consequences.
