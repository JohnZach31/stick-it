### What your notice must include

A written notice should contain: (1) your physical or electronic signature; (2) identification of the copyrighted work you say was infringed; (3) identification of the material and **where it is** (for Stick-It, the share link or page address); (4) your contact details (address, telephone, e-mail); (5) a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law; and (6) a statement, **under penalty of perjury**, that the information is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.

Please be accurate. Knowingly misrepresenting that material is infringing can carry legal consequences.
