# Accessibility (Israel) - draft

Israeli rules: the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law 5758-1998, the Service Accessibility Regulations 5773-2013, and Israeli Standard SI 5568 (web content accessibility, based on WCAG). The regulations require certain service providers to make websites accessible and to publish an **accessibility statement** with a named **accessibility coordinator** and contact route. Whether and when this applies to a non-commercial individual operator is **UNVERIFIED**; counsel should confirm.

## What exists
- Technical target WCAG 2.2 AA; details in `accessibility.md`. **No conformance claim and no certificate.**
- English and Hebrew accessibility statements (`legal/accessibility.html`, `legal/he/accessibility.html`) with honest limitations.
- Hebrew pages are right-to-left with the self-hosted Heebo font; 200% zoom and 375px width checked.

## Owner actions
1. Decide whether to appoint an accessibility coordinator (name and contact) and add the details to the statement.
2. Configure the accessibility/support e-mail.
3. Consider an audit by an accredited Israeli accessibility specialist and screen-reader testing before public launch.
4. Do not claim "fully accessible" or "SI 5568 compliant" unless an audit supports it.
