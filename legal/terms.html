  • 13 to 17 (teen): you may use Stick-It. Your account starts with conservative settings: no product-update e-mails, share links that show no profile details by default, and no public profile. 7. Sharing, public links and collaboration

  • A share link shows what you shared to anyone who has the link, without an account. Links stay active until you turn them off. A snapshot link shows a frozen copy. A live board link shows the whole board, read-only, as it changes.
  • What a link shows about you (name, picture, bio) is what you chose in Account settings when the link was created.
  • Collaborators you invite can see, and with an editor role change, the board. Decide carefully who you invite.
  • People you send a link to can keep copies. We cannot take back what others have saved.
