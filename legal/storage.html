Storage notice (DRAFT)

Draft for review. [LEGAL REVIEW RECOMMENDED]

Stick-It sets no cookies and uses no advertising, analytics or session-recording tools. It uses your browser's own storage (local storage and IndexedDB) only because the app needs it to work:

WhatWhy
Your guest boards and notesSo your work is still there next time
Settings (theme, fonts, preferences)To remember your choices
The result of the age step (adult or teen, with a timestamp, never a birth date); it is kept for up to 90 days on a browser where you have signed inSo you are not asked again each time you sign in
When signed in: your sign-in session and a cached copy of your boardsTo sign you in and show boards quickly or offline

This storage is strictly needed to provide what you asked for. Clearing your browser data removes it, and signing out removes the signed-in copy. Guest boards are not sent to us; clearing browser data deletes them for good unless you exported them.

See the Privacy Policy.
