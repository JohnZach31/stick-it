Young people and parents (DRAFT)

Draft for review. Not legal advice and not final. [LEGAL REVIEW RECOMMENDED]

Stick-It asks "How old are you?" before it offers sign-in. We keep only the result, never your date of birth.

What each age group can do

AgeWhat happens
18 or olderFull use, with or without an account.
13 to 17Accounts are allowed with conservative settings: no product-update e-mail, share links show no profile details by default, no public profile.
Under 13Guest use only. Boards stay in the browser on that device. A cloud account, sharing and other cloud features need verified consent from a parent or guardian.

Parental consent: honest status

A verified parental-consent process does not exist yet. Until it does, children under 13 can use Stick-It only as guests. We will not pretend a click or an e-mail address is verified consent.

If a child's cloud account was created anyway (for example by getting around the age step), the account is restricted and kept pending: cloud features are blocked, we do not delete it automatically, and a parent or guardian can ask us to delete it. Accounts that never finish the age step and have nothing in them are cleaned up by a scheduled job once the owner turns that job on.

What we do not do for young people

  • No marketing e-mail to anyone under 18.
  • No public profiles by default.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • No collection of a date of birth.

For parents and guardians

To ask what we hold about a child, or to ask us to delete a child's account, write to [OWNER INPUT REQUIRED: privacy e-mail]. Please tell us which sign-in account it is. We may need to confirm that you are the parent or guardian.
